# Route selection

Choose the route from the kind of reusable decision being saved, not from a folder name.

## VOX paper collage

Use this route when the reusable unit is a paper-cut visual system:

- detachable subjects, props, foreground or background layers;
- cutout, overlap, depth, shadow, reveal, and motion relationships;
- scene prompts that describe a stable beginning state and a limited action;
- shot, layer, and handoff contracts.

Keep a record focused on the role the element can play again. A paper airplane is not reusable because it is an airplane; it is reusable because its layers, reveal, scale, depth, and motion can carry a new concept without inheriting an old topic.

## Editorial magazine explainer

Use this route when the reusable unit makes a fact, relationship, or explanation readable:

- phones, browser cards, charts, lists, tickets, labels, diagrams, and other semantic components;
- component-contained text rules, masks, perspective, internal safe areas, and parent-child motion;
- caption layers and safe-zone behavior;
- deterministic Remotion structures and scene contracts.

Keep the visual mechanics and remove the original factual claim. A reusable quota bar is a structure for comparing quantities, not a reusable set of admissions numbers.

## Handoff to the production Skill

After selecting records from the editorial route, pass these facts into the production task:

1. asset_id and file location;
2. the component's purpose;
3. reuse conditions and prohibited uses;
4. the new topic's facts, evidence, and caption source;
5. whether the component has internal editable text.

Then use the [Editorial Magazine Explainer Producer](https://github.com/JasperMZ-Zcy/muzhi-editorial-magazine-skill) to build the actual project. The production Skill remains responsible for the master clock, scene plan, revision locks, rendering, and final QA.
